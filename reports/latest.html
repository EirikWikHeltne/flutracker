Key findings this week

•Norway: 171 lab-confirmed influenza cases in week 42 (+8% on the week before). That is 97% above the average for the same week in the previous 4 seasons (index 197), and higher than in any previous season. Intensity: baseline.
•Sweden: 81 lab-confirmed influenza cases in week 42 (+62% on the week before). That is 153% above the average for the same week in the previous 4 seasons (index 253), and higher than in any previous season. Intensity: baseline.
•Denmark: 87 lab-confirmed influenza cases in week 42 (+18% on the week before). That is 91% above the average for the same week in the previous 4 seasons (index 191). Intensity: baseline.
•Denmark is above ECDC's epidemic threshold: 12.2% of sentinel samples were positive for influenza in week 42.
•Highest influenza level against history: Sweden (index 253, where 100 = historical average).
•Dominant influenza virus so far this season (subtyped samples, all three countries): A(H1N1)pdm09 (84%).
•COVID-19, lab-confirmed cases this week: Norway 167 (+8%, index 27); Sweden 85 (−30%, index 6); Denmark 56 (−5%, index 3).
•Hospital and severity this week: Sweden 2 deaths with influenza; Sweden 6 deaths with COVID-19.
