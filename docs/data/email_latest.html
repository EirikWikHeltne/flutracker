Worth knowing

•Norway is above ECDC's epidemic threshold: 24.0% of sentinel samples tested positive for influenza in week 46.
•Norway has the most influenza relative to a normal season: 14% of an average season peak.
•A(H1N1)pdm09 accounts for 90% of subtyped influenza samples this season, across the Nordic countries.
•Sweden reported 5 deaths with influenza and 10 deaths with COVID-19 and Denmark reported 9.7 hospitalisations for severe respiratory infection (SARI) this week.

How to read intensity

Take the busiest week of each earlier season and average them. This week’s cases ÷ that average peak is the intensity. It shows how close we are to the top of a normal season.
